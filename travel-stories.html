Paris - Eiffel Tower

Paris is the capital city of France and is known for its art, architecture, food, and history. The Eiffel Tower is one of the city's most recognizable landmarks and a popular attraction for visitors.

Rome - Colosseum

Rome is the capital of Italy and is famous for its ancient history and architecture. The Colosseum is an ancient Roman amphitheater and one of Rome's best-known landmarks.

New York - Statue of Liberty

New York City is a major city in the United States. The Statue of Liberty is located on Liberty Island and is one of the city's most recognized landmarks.
